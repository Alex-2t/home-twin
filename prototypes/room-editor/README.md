# Sketch: plan editor, “Rooms”, “Furniture” and “Lights” modes

**Historical sketch.** Product development continues in [editor/](../../editor/README.md). The capabilities, measurements and lists of what is missing below refer to this sketch; the current queue is in [status.md](../../docs/status.md).

A check (2026-11-03): is it convenient to edit the plan by hand, and how often does the geometry break. On the left, the plan from above with the underlay; on the right, live 3D. The user draws rooms, the code builds the walls (as in [walls-from-json](../walls-from-json/README.md)).

## How to build and open
```
node build.mjs                      # → index.html (~0.9 MB)
python3 -m http.server 8765         # from the prototypes folder
# http://localhost:8765/room-editor/index.html
node fuzz.mjs 3000 [seed]           # run the generator on random plans
```
Takes from `3d_floor` (read-only): the room outlines from `devices.json`, the plan photo `apartment/reference/plan-horizontal.png` for the underlay, three and esbuild. Openings come from `walls-from-json/plan.js`, `polygon-clipping` from `walls-from-json/node_modules` (here `node_modules` is a link to it).
The underlay is embedded into `index.html` and `underlay.gen.js`; both are in `.gitignore`, so the personal plan does not get into the repository.

## What it can do
- Rectangle and polygon; dragging a corner, a wall or a whole room.
- Snapping: to a 5 cm grid and to the x/y of other rooms' corners, with guides. Shift — no snapping.
- A wall shared by rooms moves as a whole: all sides on the same line, and neighbours' parallel sides closer than 45 cm, along a chain. Alt — drag only your own side.
- Double-click a wall — enter its length in centimetres: the next wall shifts in parallel together with its neighbours, right angles stay right. Double-click a room — its name.
- Alt+click a wall — a new corner; Delete — delete a corner or a room.
- Doors and windows are attached to a room side and move with the wall; if the side becomes shorter than the opening, the opening is hidden.
- Validation: self-intersection, a side shorter than 5 cm, an area smaller than 0.25 m², rooms overlapping each other. Such rooms turn red, 3D is not updated, and the reason is shown at the bottom.
- Undo and redo (⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z), autosave in the browser, “Download JSON”.
- Underlay: opacity, dragging, scaling by two points and a known distance.

## “Furniture” mode (2026-11-03)
- Catalog on the left: 4 items generated by code (`furniture.js`: wardrobe, shelving unit, bed, nightstand — any size, 4 colours) and 13 Kenney models (CC0, fixed size). The catalog pictures are rendered from the models themselves on load.
- An item is dragged from the catalog onto the plan. Next to a wall it stands with its back to it, facing into the room; in a corner it sits flush against the neighbouring wall; away from walls it snaps to the grid. R — rotate by 90°, Shift+R — by 15°, Delete — delete, Shift — no snapping.
- Sized furniture has width and depth handles on the plan, W×D×H fields in the item card, and a colour.
- Validation: overlaps other furniture, blocks a door (a 70 cm zone on both sides), goes into a wall — the item turns red, the reason is at the bottom.
- In 3D, furniture is updated separately from the walls (only its position while dragging). Tall furniture with its back to the camera is hidden, like the facade.
- In JSON: `furniture: [{model, x, y, rot, w, d, h, color}]`.
- Style conclusion: code-generated furniture looks neat and matches the walls; Kenney next to it looks toy-like.

## “Lights” mode (2026-11-03)
- On the left, a list of lamps “as if from Home Assistant”: 14 lamps from `devices.json` with name, entity and area, but without coordinates. Lamps without a place are highlighted.
- A lamp is dragged onto the plan. A point lands on the ceiling and snaps to the room centre when near it; dragging it from the list again adds another point. A strip lies along the whole nearest wall of the room with a 10 cm inset from the corners; again — onto the next wall. The strip ends can be dragged along the wall.
- Height via presets: ceiling, pendant, sconce; cornice, worktop, plinth (guessed from the lamp name). Colour, “2×2 grid” for spots, “Remove from plan”.
- 💡 in the list turns the lamp on and off, as in HA. A “Day / Night” button.
- 3D lighting is computed by the card's code (`lighting.js`, `room-light-mask.js`), unchanged; the room mask is reinstalled after every edit (materials get their original hooks back).
- Measurement on an M4 Pro: a new lamp freezes the view for 0.45–0.67 s — the number of light sources changes and all shaders are recompiled. A second point of the same chandelier takes 26 ms (one source per group). On a phone it will take longer. Fix: keep a constant set of sources with zero intensity in advance so their number never changes.

## What was verified
- A full 3D rebuild takes 6–25 ms, on every mouse move; 2D walls take 4 ms on my apartment.
- `fuzz.mjs`, 3 runs of 3000 random edits of my apartment and 3000 random plans from scratch: 2 geometry failures out of ~5700 valid plans of my apartment (0.04%), 0 on plans from scratch. On a failure, the last successful 3D stays.
- Found and fixed by the run: an opening on a shortened wall produced a NaN point and broke the walls in 9% of edits; a rare `polygon-clipping` failure to close an outline is fixed by retrying with 2 mm and 5 mm rounding.
- Bugs found in the browser and fixed: the rectangle did not draw; the kids' room wall did not drag the hallway (walls are now linked along a chain); the hint line changed height and the plan jumped.

## What is missing
“Doors and windows” mode and devices other than lights; curved walls; multiple floors; phone (mouse only).

## To do later (the owner's feedback 2026-11-03: the concept works)
- Borrow from NeonPlan (D-013, D-014): a “+” in the middle of a side for a new corner, arrow keys for nudging; a room by dimensions and X/Y/width/depth fields — only hidden, for rare cases.
- Walls: “remove wall” in plain sight; thickness and height of an individual wall — hidden (D-014). Sloped ceilings — later, for houses and attics.
- Furniture: glows according to entity state (bed occupied, TV on); ready-made models — scale within ±20% so they don't get distorted.
- “Doors and windows” mode, curtains, air conditioners and other devices.
- Lights: lamps on furniture (floor lamp, sconce), a strip that wraps around a corner in one motion, binding to real HA entities, the freeze when adding a lamp (see above).
- Furniture: kitchen along a wall, sofas and upholstered furniture in our own style (only Kenney for now), rugs; furniture doesn't move with the wall; on overlap there's no hint where to move it.
- Phone and tablet: touch, large handles, two-finger pan.
- Snapping to wall thickness: a new room should sit 10–25 cm from its neighbour, not flush against it.
- An underlay shift is saved silently: the plan looks crooked although it is the photo that moved (happened 2026-11-03). Needs an explicit “underlay moved” indicator or locking after alignment. For now there is the “Reset underlay” button.
- Underlay by four points: the sheet photo is taken at an angle, so with two points the plan drifts by centimetres.
- A new corner without Alt (e.g. a handle in the middle of a side), hints for beginners.
- The remaining rare `polygon-clipping` failures (2 in 5700 plans): retry with a sub-millimetre shift, or a different library.
- Curved walls, multiple floors.
- Where the editor lives: a website or a card (O-001).
