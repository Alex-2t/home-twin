# Roadmap

Started 2026-11-17. A public roadmap for the articles about Home Twin: what comes after the first release, written so a reader understands it without the decision journal. It collects candidates as they come up; the order and the wording are settled when an article goes out.

An item here is a direction, not a promise or a date.

## Candidates

### More than one floor

Two-storey houses, a flat on two levels, a basement or an attic: switch floors in the plan and in 3D, see the whole house with the floors stacked, a staircase as part of the structure. The AI build tells the floors apart from the drawing and the video.

Today: one floor per plan, one ceiling height per plan (D-090); a multi-floor plan is told apart and not built (first release is single-floor).

### The outside: the building and the land around it

The house seen from the street: facade, roof, the plot with a garden, a driveway, a fence, a terrace, a pool. Outdoor lights, gates, a garage door, irrigation and outdoor sensors from Home Assistant placed where they really are.

Today: lawn and gravel as floor coverings, a terrace railing (D-071); no roof, facade or plot.

### The real sun and the weather outside

The light in the model follows the real time of day and the home's orientation: the sun moves across the rooms, clouds dim it, rain shows on the windows, night comes when it comes outside. Uses the location and weather Home Assistant already has.

Today: a manual day / night switch; weather is shown in the home panel only.

### The home's day, replayed

A timeline under the 3D: drag it back and watch the last hours or days play out — which lights were on, when the doors and windows opened, where there was motion, how the temperature moved room by room. Built from the history Home Assistant keeps; nothing new to record.

Today: charts per room in the home panel; no replay in the model.

### Climate and energy on the plan

A heat map on the floor: temperature, humidity or CO₂ room by room, the cold corner and the stuffy bedroom visible at a glance; energy use drawn on the devices that use it.

Today: readings as numbers on the walls (D-034); statistics in the home panel.

### People and motion in the model

Presence and motion sensors light up the rooms where someone is; a robot vacuum moves on the plan along its real path; a camera opens from where it hangs in the room.

Today: cameras and the vacuum in the home panel, not placed in the model.
